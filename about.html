ℹ️ About us

🧠 What is Quiz Maker?

Quiz Maker turns a simple list of questions and answers into a fun, interactive quiz. Paste your list, choose your settings and play, with no sign-up and nothing to install.

🎯 Why we built it

We wanted a fast way to host weekly quizzes for a YouTube audience, with a timer, emojis and a design that looks great on any screen.

✨ What you can do

📝Make a quizPaste a list and play instantly.
⏱️Timer and lifelinesStreaks, points, 50/50 and skip.
🔗Share linkSend a quiz-only link to participants.
🎥Stream modeBigger text for screen recording.
🔒PrivateNo accounts. Your questions stay in your browser.

🔒 Your privacy

There are no accounts. Your questions stay in your browser, or inside the link you choose to share.
